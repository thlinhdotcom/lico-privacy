Privacy Policy

Lico Water Sort · Developer: LinhTH
Effective: October 17, 2026

About this policy

LinhTH develops Lico Water Sort, listed on Google Play as "Lico Water Sort: Color Puzzle". This policy explains how the Android game handles information. Contact: thlinh.com@gmail.com. Effective date: October 17, 2026.

Information handled by the game

The game does not ask you to create an account. It does not collect or transmit personal information to the developer or to advertising or analytics SDKs. It does not request access to contacts, precise location, photos, microphone or camera. There are no ads, in-app purchases, analytics SDKs or developer-operated game servers in this version.

Local game saves and preferences

The game stores your current puzzle, bottle contents, move and undo history, level progress, records, stars, daily-puzzle results, unlocked themes and settings in its private storage on your device. These records let you continue playing and keep your preferences. The app does not send these records to LinhTH. Android backup or device-transfer services may copy app data according to your device settings and the service provider's own policies. LinhTH does not operate or have access to those backups.

Permissions, audio and network access

The app uses the Android vibration permission for optional haptic feedback. You can turn haptics, sound effects and background music off in Settings. The game itself does not request the Android Internet permission. Its puzzles, music and built-in privacy policy work offline.

If you contact support

If you choose to email thlinh.com@gmail.com, we receive the email address, message and any attachments you send. We use that information to answer your request and resolve support issues, not for advertising. Email delivery is also handled by your and our email providers under their respective policies. Do not include sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your request. We retain support correspondence only as needed to handle the issue or meet applicable legal obligations.

Sharing and platform services

The game does not sell or share personal information with advertisers or data brokers. Google Play, Android and your device manufacturer may separately process installation, diagnostics, backup or device-service information under their own policies. This policy describes the game and developer support; it does not replace those platform policies.

Retention and deletion

Local game records remain on the device until you clear app storage or uninstall the game, subject to Android backup and restore behavior. To remove local records, open Android Settings, Apps, Lico Water Sort, Storage, and choose Clear storage or Clear data. This resets progress. The game has no account to delete. To ask us to delete support correspondence, email thlinh.com@gmail.com. You can manage operating-system backups in your device or cloud-account settings.

Children's privacy

The game has no account system, chat, advertising or feature designed to request children's personal information. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through gameplay. A parent or guardian who believes a child has sent personal information to support can contact thlinh.com@gmail.com to request deletion.

Changes and contact

If the game's information practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date. Developer: LinhTH. Support and privacy contact: thlinh.com@gmail.com.
